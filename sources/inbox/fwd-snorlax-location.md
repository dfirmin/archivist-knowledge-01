Answering the question from standup. In the original Kanto games there are exactly two Snorlax and they're not in the grass: one is asleep blocking Route 12 and the other blocks Route 16. You can't get past either until you have the Poké Flute (Mr. Fuji gives it to you in Lavender Town after you clear Pokémon Tower). Play it next to the Snorlax and it wakes up and battles you, so save first if you want to catch it.

That's it, there's no wild Snorlax anywhere else in Kanto as far as I know.
