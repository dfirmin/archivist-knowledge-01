notes - Zoroark (for the Unova page)

- how do you get it: in Black/White Zorua wasn't in the wild, it came from a special event (you had to bring an event Celebi), then Zorua evolves into Zoroark at level 30
- in later Unova games you can find Zorua in the wild, I *think* in a house in Driftveil but double check that
- Illusion ability: it looks like the last Pokémon in your party until it gets hit
- dark type, so Fighting, Bug and Fairy moves are bad news for it
