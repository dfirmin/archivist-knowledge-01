# Field notes: Voltrisk (Aurelia region)

From the Aurelia Regional Pokédex survey, entry #001 (National Pokédex #1026). Aurelia is a newly charted
island region; its Pokémon were first catalogued in Generation X.

Voltrisk is an Electric/Steel-type Pokémon. Surveyors found it nesting in the copper cliffs above
Brightwater Bay, where storms are frequent.

Base stats recorded by the survey team:

- HP 70
- Attack 95
- Defense 85
- Sp. Atk 110
- Sp. Def 80
- Speed 120

Total: 560

Abilities: Static, and Lightning Rod as its hidden ability.

Size: 1.1 m tall, 42.0 kg.

Evolution: Voltrisk evolves from Sparkit at level 32. It does not evolve further.

Type matchups noted by the team: weak to Ground (4x) and Fighting and Fire (2x). Immune to Poison.
Resists Flying, Steel, Bug, Rock, Grass, Psychic, Ice, Dragon, Fairy and Normal.

No one has written up how trainers use it in battle yet.
