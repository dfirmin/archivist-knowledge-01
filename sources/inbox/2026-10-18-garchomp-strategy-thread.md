ok so after a month of running him on the ladder here's where I landed. Garchomp is a fast physical sweeper, plain and simple. base 130 attack and 102 speed means he outspeeds most of what he needs to hit and Earthquake + Dragon Claw covers almost everything (Dragon/Ground STAB is a ridiculous combo).

typical set people run: Earthquake, Dragon Claw or Outrage, Stone Edge for flyers, Swords Dance. Rough Skin is the ability you want, contact attackers chip themselves.

what beats him: anything icy. he's 4x weak to Ice so even a weak Ice Beam from a special attacker deletes him. Fairy types wall the Dragon moves too.
