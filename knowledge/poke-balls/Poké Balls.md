# Poké Balls

A Poké Ball is the item Trainers throw to catch wild Pokémon. The better the ball, the better the chance of a catch. This folder covers the four main balls, from the most basic to the best.

| Ball | Catch rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Poké Ball](Poké%20Ball.md) | Standard | The basic ball, sold in shops |
| [Great Ball](Great%20Ball.md) | Better than a Poké Ball | Sold in shops |
| [Ultra Ball](Ultra%20Ball.md) | Better than a Great Ball | Sold in shops |
| [Master Ball](Master%20Ball.md) | Never fails | Not sold; usually one per game |

Many other balls exist, such as the Premier Ball, Timer Ball and Dusk Ball. They are not covered here yet.
