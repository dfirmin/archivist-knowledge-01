# Great Ball

## Overview

The Great Ball is the next step up from the Poké Ball. It has been there since Generation I.

## Catch Rate

It has a catch rate multiplier of 1.5x, so it works better than a Poké Ball.

## How to Get It

It is sold in Poké Marts, usually once the player has earned a few Gym badges.
