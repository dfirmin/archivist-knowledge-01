# Master Ball

## Overview

The Master Ball is the ultimate Poké Ball. It has been there since Generation I.

## Catch Rate

It never fails to catch a wild Pokémon. It cannot be used to catch a Pokémon that belongs to another Trainer.

## How to Get It

It is not sold in shops. In most games the player receives one as a special gift or reward, so it is worth saving for a rare or legendary Pokémon.
