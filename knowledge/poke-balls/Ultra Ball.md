# Ultra Ball

## Overview

The Ultra Ball is the best ball that can normally be bought in a shop. It has been there since Generation I.

## Catch Rate

It has a catch rate multiplier of 2x, twice that of a Poké Ball.

## How to Get It

It is sold in Poké Marts later in the game, and costs more than a Great Ball.
