# Poké Ball

## Overview

The Poké Ball is the basic ball in the Pokémon games. It has been there since Generation I and is the ball most Trainers start with.

## Catch Rate

It has a catch rate multiplier of 1x, which is the baseline. Every other standard ball is measured against it.

## How to Get It

It is sold in Poké Marts and is often given to the player early in the game.
