# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses the company reimburses and how to claim them.

*Source: [Expense Reimbursement Policy](sources/processed/expense-reimbursement-policy.md), retrieved 2026-10-05*

## Who It Applies To

All employees. Contractors are reimbursed under the terms of their contracts. The November 1 meal cap change does not change this for contractors; they follow their contracts.

*Source: [Expense Reimbursement Policy](sources/processed/expense-reimbursement-policy.md), retrieved 2026-10-05*
*Source: [Meal Limit Change](sources/processed/fwd-meal-limit-change.md), retrieved 2026-10-05*

## Rules

1. Submit claims in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense.
2. Attach an itemized receipt for every expense over $25.
3. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $75 per day (raised from $60 per day, effective November 1).
4. Alcohol is not reimbursed.
5. Claims over $1,000 need approval from the employee's director before submission.

The rest of the policy, including the receipts rule, is unchanged by the meal cap amendment.

## Exceptions
