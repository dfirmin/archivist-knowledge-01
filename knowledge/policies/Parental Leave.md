## Purpose

This policy provides paid time off for employees to care for a new child following birth, adoption, or foster placement.

*Source: [Re: Re: parental leave update?? (pls read)](sources/processed/re-parental-leave-update.md), retrieved 2026-10-04*

## Who It Applies To

Starting January 1, 2027, all employees are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave (increased from 12 weeks). This leave applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement. Contractors are not covered by this policy and remain on their agency's terms.

*Source: [Re: Re: parental leave update?? (pls read)](sources/processed/re-parental-leave-update.md), retrieved 2026-10-04*

## Rules

Employees receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The leave must be taken within 12 months of the child arriving. Employees can split the leave into up to 2 blocks. Employees must notify their manager and People Operations at least 4 weeks before taking leave, unless it is an emergency.

*Source: [Re: Re: parental leave update?? (pls read)](sources/processed/re-parental-leave-update.md), retrieved 2026-10-04*

## Exceptions

The Chief People Officer can approve exceptions to the policy when unusual circumstances arise (for example, twins plus a move abroad).
